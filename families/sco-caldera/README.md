# SCO / Caldera / UnixWare / OpenServer

This family covers commercial Unix licensing and source-release material associated with the Santa Cruz Operation, Caldera, UnixWare, OpenServer, and related Unix ownership and licensing history.

## Scope

This family covers:

- SCO OpenServer licensing context;
- UnixWare licensing context;
- Caldera source-release material;
- SCO and Caldera public source or historical source releases;
- Unix/Linux dispute-related licensing material where it is useful to the licensing history.

## Research questions

- Which SCO/Caldera materials were genuine open source, which were source-available, and which remained proprietary?
- What precise terms applied to Caldera's ancient Unix source releases?
- How did SCO/Caldera licensing history interact with AT&T/USL/Novell Unix ownership claims?

## Collection policy

Treat this family conservatively. Do not reproduce proprietary or dispute-related documents unless redistribution status is clear. Prefer metadata-only entries with strong provenance notes until specific primary documents are reviewed.
