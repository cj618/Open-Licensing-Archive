# AT&T / Bell Labs / Western Electric / USL / Novell Unix

This family covers the source-access and proprietary licensing history around early Unix and commercial Unix before and during the emergence of BSD, GNU, Linux, and open source.

## Scope

This family covers:

- Bell Labs and Western Electric Unix notices;
- AT&T source access terms;
- UNIX System Laboratories material;
- System III and System V licensing context;
- Novell-era Unix ownership and licensing material;
- source-available but non-open-source Unix agreements;
- historically important redistribution, research, education, and confidentiality restrictions.

## Research questions

- What documents controlled early research and educational access to Unix source?
- Which terms restricted redistribution, publication, sublicensing, or derivative works?
- How did AT&T encumbrances shape the BSD licensing story?
- Which later documents are safe to reproduce, and which should remain metadata-only?

## Collection policy

Treat early Unix and proprietary Unix documents conservatively. Unless a primary source clearly permits redistribution, entries in this family should be metadata-only with strong source notes rather than full text.
